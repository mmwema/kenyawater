### **Access to Water Via Public Tap in Kenya**

A public tap is a tap that is located at a central position so that it can serve as many
people as possible within a particular area. **8.6% of Kenyan households access their water through a public tap.**

- **Most of the Kenyan counties (42 out of 47) ranked Very Low** for the number of households that accessed water through public taps.

- Top counties for access to water via public tap were **Kilifi (36%) Kisumu (22.1%), Turkana (21.2%), Mombasa (20.5%) and Taita Taveta (20.3%) counties.**

- **Kisii (1%) and Vihiga (1.6%) and Wajir (1.6%)** had the lowest number of households getting their water via public tap.

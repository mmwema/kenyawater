### Water Access Via Water Vendors in Kenya

**Only 6.2% of Kenyan households rely on water vendors** for their main water supply. This is not surprising given that water vendors can be
costly

- **In 45 of the 47 Kenyan counties, household reliance on water vendors was
  very low**. Even in the counties with high reliance on water vendors, the number of households relying on vendors was low.

- **Mombasa county** (with 43.4% of households relying on water vendors) and **Kajiado county** (with 23.3%) had the highest reliance on water
  vendors.

- **Elgeyo Marakwet (0.3)%, Nyamira (0.4%) and Kisii (0.5%)** counties had the lowest number of households relying on water vendors for water.

### **Piped Water Usage in Kenya**

Piped water statistics include both water piped into the dwelling and water piped into the yard (or compound). Only about **17.9% of Kenyan households accessed** water through piped water. Most counties in Kenya have very low access to piped water.

- **Nyeri county (at 64.3%) had the highest** number of households access water through piped water while **Marsabit and Nyamira had the lowest at 1.9% each.**

- **No Kenyan county had a score of over 80% access** (very high) while only one
  **(Nyeri)** had high. 6 counties had average, 8 had low and the majority (32 counties) had very low.

- **Counties in the central part of Kenya** (such as *Nyeri, Meru, Embu, Tharaka Nithi* and *Nairobi)* led other counties in access to piped water. **Counties in northern Kenya and Nyanza** had the lowest access.

### **Bottled Water Usage in Kenya**

Reliance on bottled water for water needs was very low in Kenya **with only 1.3% of Kenyan households relying on bottled water.** Bottled water is quite expensive and is therefore out of reach of many Kenyans.

- **All 47 counties fell in the Very Low category** for bottled water reliance.

-  **Kajiado (11.9%) and Mombasa (11.5%) counties** had the largest number of households relying on bottled water.

-  **Half the counties in Kenya had less than 0.6% of households** relying on bottled water with **Turkana county having the least at 0.1% of households.**

### **Surface Water Usage in Kenya**

Using surface water (rivers, lakes, natural springs and ponds) was the most common
way that Kenyan households accessed water with **an average of 38.9% of households** in Kenya getting their water this way.

- **Kisii county (at 87.1%) and Nyamira (83.4%) had the highest** number of households getting their water from surface water while **Nairobi county (at 0.6%) had the least.**

- **3 counties in Kenya had very high usage of surface water**, 4 had high, 13 had average, 17 had low and 10 had very low.

- As expected **counties with large urban centers** (like *Nairobi, Mombasa and Kiambu*) had low usage of surface water.

- **Counties in the west of Kenya** generally had high usage of surface water due to good rainfall and availability of rivers and lakes.

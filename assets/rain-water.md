### **Rain Water Harvesting in Kenya**

Despite the fact that 20-30% of Kenya gets reliable rainfall, rain water harvesting is
surprisingly low. **Only 4% of Kenya's households harvest rain water.** This could be due to the huge cost of buying or building water tanks and the associated infrastructure.

- **All 47 Kenyan counties ranked very low** (less than 20% of households) for rain water harvesting and usage.

- **Homa Bay (15.5%), Bomet (13.8%) and Nakuru (13.4%)** has the highest number of households relying on rain water for their water needs.

- **Tana River (0.1%) and Turkana county (0.2%), had the lowest number** of households relying on rain water.

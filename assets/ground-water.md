### **Ground Water Usage in Kenya**

Ground water (such as wells and boreholes) was the second most popular way that
Kenyan's got access to water (after surface water). **23% of Kenyan's accessed water through ground water.**

- Despite being the second most popular way of accessing water, **no county in Kenya was in the High or Very High category**. 6 counties were in the average category, 19 in the low and 22 in the very low.

- **Wajir (51.6%) and Marsabit (50.3%) had the greatest number of households** getting their water through ground water. Both these counties are in the arid north part of Kenya.

- **Nyeri county (at 4.3%) had the lowest number of households** getting their water via ground water. This is probably because most of these households get their water from piped water.
